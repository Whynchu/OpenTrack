## Ruler edge auto-scroll — source iteration 4

The user requested horizontal scrolling when a ruler drag reaches the left or right edge. In `app.rs::show_project`, horizontal seek drags now use a 32 px edge zone with a quadratic speed ramp capped at 720 logical pixels per second. The rate uses egui's frame delta, is clamped at the scrollable timeline bounds, and requests a short repaint while movement continues. Playhead calculation accounts for the scroll delta in the same frame so the marker remains under the pointer. Edge scrolling is suppressed during the existing vertical-drag zoom gesture.

Acceptance is still pending: hold a horizontal ruler drag at each edge and confirm bars scroll smoothly while the marker stays under the pointer; verify the left boundary stops at bar 1 and the right edge continues beyond bar 50; then confirm ordinary horizontal seeking and vertical zoom anchoring remain intact.
